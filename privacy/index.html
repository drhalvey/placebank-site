Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Place Bank is made by Ed Halvey, an individual developer in Perth, Western Australia. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you save is sold or shared for marketing. This page explains exactly where your information goes.

What stays on your iPhone

Your places, trips, photos, notes, ratings and "been" ticks are stored on your iPhone. If you never turn on sharing, they never leave it except for the look-ups described below.

Sharing, only if you turn it on

FeatureWhat is sentWho can see it
Shared listYour places, trips, photos and the first name you enter, stored under a secret 10-letter code.Anyone you give the code to.
Group tripThat trip and the places saved in its city. Your own notes, ratings and ticks are not sent.Anyone you give the trip code to.
Share linkA copy of one place or trip (name, address, description, hours, photos). Never your notes, ratings or name.Anyone with the link, on placebank.app.

Shared data is stored with Supabase in Sydney, Australia. You can leave a shared list or group trip at any time in the app. To have a list, trip or link deleted from the server, email the address below.

Reading what you save (AI)

To work out which places a clip, article, screenshot or description means, Place Bank first uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone where available, which stays on the device. Otherwise the text or picture is sent through Place Bank's server to Google's Gemini AI, and for descriptions of recent or half-remembered places, Google Search. YouTube links are read by Google through a separate Place Bank server (Cloudflare). Google may use content sent on its free service to improve its products, so please do not save anything private or sensitive. A random ID for your install, not linked to you, is used to apply daily limits, and the number of requests is counted.

Finding places, photos and opening hours

Place names and addresses are looked up on Apple Maps. Opening hours and features come from the venue's own website, OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia. Those services receive the place name or location being looked up, not who you are.

Your location

If you allow it, your location is used on your iPhone to show distances, journey times and nearby alerts. It is never sent to Place Bank's server. Directions and journey times come from Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Place Bank is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. Questions, or a request to see or delete anything held on the server: ejhalvey@gmail.com.
