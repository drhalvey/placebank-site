Everything you saved, right where you are

Open a city when you land and every place you ever meant to try is there, on a map and in a list.

Save from anywhere

Clips, articles, screenshots, map links or a half-remembered description. It reads them and picks out each place.

On the map, by city

Addresses checked on Apple Maps, with photos, website, reviews and opening hours. London, Perth, wherever.

Nearby nudges

Walk past somewhere you saved and your phone tells you, so the good ones stop slipping by.

Trips that plan themselves

Pick your dates. It splits your places into days by area, finds stays near them and shows how to get round.

Share with your people

One list with your partner, or a trip code for friends. Send any place as a link that opens without the app.

Tick off where you've been

Rate it, add what you had, and keep a map of everywhere you've actually made it to.
