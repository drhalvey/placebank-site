How it works

Share it in. Place Bank does the rest.

Tap Share in any app and pick Place Bank, paste a link, or just describe it in your own words. It works out where you mean.

STEP · SAV
01

Save

From TikTok, Instagram, Safari or Maps. Or type "the Thai place near the markets".

IN ▸ clip · link · words
STEP · CHK
02

It checks

Finds the real venue, confirms the address on Apple Maps, adds photos, website and opening hours.

GATE ▸ address verified
STEP · ARR
03

You arrive

Open the city and it's all there on a map, with a nudge when you walk past one.

OUT ▸ Perth · London · anywhere

Why it's different

Built for the moment you get there.

Saved posts get lost in a feed. Place Bank turns them into a map you actually use.

Save from anywhere

Clips, articles, screenshots, map links or a ramble. It picks out every place mentioned.

Addresses checked

Nearby nudges

Open now?
